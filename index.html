Who We Are

One Platform. Three Corridors. Zero Friction.

GAC Global is a pan-African workforce mobility company headquartered in Bilbao, Spain, with an operational hub in Lobito, Angola. We integrate legal compliance, HSSE protocols, and human mobility logistics under a single platform — replacing five fragmented providers with one accountable partner.

We were built to answer one question: why does it take so long to get a technical specialist from a European office to an African job site, ready to work? GAC OS — our proprietary operating system — makes that journey predictable, documented, and measurable.

Our founding node in Angola validates the model. Zambia and Namibia follow the same playbook — adapting to local regulations, fleet realities, and client ecosystems already familiar to us from Angola.

The Lobito Corridor

Three Nodes. One Integrated Network.

Each node is a fully operational GAC hub — built on the same standards, the same platform, and the same commitment to continuity. Angola validates the model. Zambia and Namibia scale it.

Live — 2026
Angola
GAC Continuity Luanda
Hub: Lobito · Luanda · Soyo · Cabinda

The founding node. Angola is where GAC validates its entire model — fleet, legal structure, HSSE protocols, GAC OS, and client relationships. Every process built here is replicated across the corridor.

Operational across Angola's four oil & gas poles: the Luanda-Lobito corridor (Corridor projects), the Cabinda offshore complex (Chevron-operated), the Soyo industrial zone (Angola LNG / Bechtel / EnerMech), and the Eastern interior (Catoca diamond operations).

Oil & Gas Mining Infrastructure Fleet-as-a-Service GAC OS Live
Launch — Q1 2027
Zambia
GAC Continuity Lusaka
Hub: Lusaka · Copperbelt

Zambia is a natural extension: many of GAC's Angola clients operate in the Copperbelt. We follow our clients into their second market — no new acquisition cost, just expanded service territory.

The Copperbelt produces ~5% of global copper output. With the green energy transition driving copper demand, Zambia will mobilise thousands of technical specialists through 2030. GAC enters at the right moment, with an established brand and an authorised immigration agent registration from day one.

Mining / Copperbelt GovTech Immigration Infrastructure Client Follow-on
Launch — Q2 2027
Namibia
GAC Continuity Windhoek
Hub: Windhoek · Walvis Bay

Namibia serves two strategic roles: Walvis Bay is the Atlantic alternative to Luanda as a logistics gateway for the SADC south corridor, and Namibia's institutional stability makes it the low-risk anchor for European clients entering the region.

GAC enters via a 51/49 JV with a local operator — reducing capital requirements and time to market while leveraging established local fleet and licences. GovTech integration active from launch via Namibia's e-services immigration platform.
